Privacy Policy

Data we collect

When you create an account on our platform, we collect certain information to provide and improve our services. This may include personal details such as your username, email address, date of birth, and profile picture. We collect data about the content you post (including text, images, and videos), as well as technical information such as your IP address, device type, browser, and activity logs. We may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyze usage patterns.

How we use your information

We use your information to operate and personalize your experience on the platform. This includes displaying your posts, managing your account, improving our recommendation systems, and ensuring community safety. We may also use your data for communication purposes, such as sending important updates or responding to support requests. Aggregated and anonymized data may be used for analytics and platform development.

Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information. However, certain data may be shared with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the platform (for example, for hosting, analytics, or content moderation). Content you choose to make public will be visible to other users. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our legal rights, or to prevent harm, fraud, or abuse on the platform.

User Content and Age-Appropriate Restrictions

Users are free to post content, provided it follows our community guidelines and remains suitable for a diverse audience. We prohibit content that is violent, sexually explicit, hateful, or otherwise inappropriate for younger users. Violations may result in content removal or account suspension. Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor their children’s use of the platform, and users under a certain age may have limited access to specific features.

Your Rights and Data Protection

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time by adjusting your account settings or contacting our support team. You can request to download your data or restrict certain processing activities. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at timeturner2025@gmail.com.
